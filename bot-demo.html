How the WhatsApp Engine Powers Ivory Dental

Designed specifically for multi-location dental practices in Singapore, this assistant converts after-hours digital interest into booked, confirmed chair time without burdening clinic staff.

Patients experiencing dental discomfort at 11:30 PM get an immediate, reassuring response instead of bouncing to a competitor clinic or postponing care indefinitely.
